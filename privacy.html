Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This website is my personal portfolio. I keep it simple: it has no accounts, no contact forms, no ads and no tracking or analytics cookies.

1. What I collect

The website itself does not ask for or store any personal information. If you contact me by email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn or YouTube, I receive whatever you choose to send — usually your name, contact details and project information.

2. How I use it

I use your message only to reply to you, prepare quotes and work on your project. I never sell, rent or share your details with third parties for marketing.

3. Services this website relies on

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record technical data such as your IP address in its server logs for security purposes.
  • Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser downloads them.
  • External links: WhatsApp, LinkedIn, YouTube and email open in their own services, which have their own privacy policies.

4. How long I keep it

I keep project emails and messages for as long as needed for the project and for my business records, then delete them when they're no longer needed.

5. Your rights

You can ask me at any time what information I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. Just email Youssef01160@gmail.com.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
