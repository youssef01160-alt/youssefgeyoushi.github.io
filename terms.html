These terms apply to freelance work — VFX compositing, AI visuals and video editing — that I, Youssef Geyoushi ("I", "me"), provide to you ("the client"). By approving a quote or sending payment, you agree to these terms. A written agreement signed by both of us takes priority over anything here.

1. Scope & quotes

Every project starts with a brief. I then send a quote that lists the shots or deliverables, the price, the number of revision rounds and the delivery date. Work outside that scope — new shots, a changed brief, a new look after approval — is quoted separately before I start it.

2. Payment

  • Unless we agree otherwise, a 50% deposit is due before work starts and the remaining 50% before the final, full-quality files are delivered.
  • For small jobs I may ask for full payment upfront.
  • Previews may be watermarked or lower resolution until the final payment is received.
  • Payment methods and currency are agreed in the quote. Any transfer fees are paid by the client.

3. Revisions

Each quote includes a set number of revision rounds (normally two). A round is one consolidated list of notes. Extra rounds, or changes that go against previously approved work, are billed at an agreed rate.

4. Timeline & delivery

Delivery dates depend on receiving the materials, feedback and payments on time. If feedback or files arrive late, the deadline moves by the same amount. Final files are delivered by download link in the format agreed in the quote. Please keep your own copy — I keep project files for 60 days after delivery, but can't guarantee them after that.

6. Ownership & usage

Once the final payment is received, you own the rights to use the final delivered files for the purpose agreed in the quote. Until then, all work remains mine. Project files, scripts, node graphs and custom AI workflows I build stay my property unless we agree in writing to hand them over.

7. Portfolio use

I may show the finished work, and breakdowns of it, on this website, in my showreel and on social media — always after the project is publicly released. If your project is under an NDA or must stay private, tell me before work starts and I won't share it.

8. Use of AI tools

Where it helps the result, I use AI tools (such as ComfyUI, Magnific, Higgsfield and Midjourney) alongside traditional compositing. I'll tell you when AI is a major part of a deliverable. If your project must avoid AI-generated content, let me know in the brief. I don't upload confidential client footage to public AI services without your permission.

9. Confidentiality

I treat your footage, scripts and project details as confidential and won't share them with anyone outside the project without your permission, except where the law requires it.

10. Cancellation

Either side can cancel in writing. The deposit covers work already done and is non-refundable once work has started. If the work completed is worth more than the deposit, the difference is billed. Files are handed over once payment for the completed work is settled.

11. Liability

I take great care with every project, but my total liability for any claim is limited to the amount you paid for that project. I'm not responsible for indirect losses, such as lost revenue or delays caused by third parties.
